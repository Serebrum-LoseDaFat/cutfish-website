How it works

From your website to a TikTok draft, in six steps.

Each step is one screen. AI does the drafting, and your team makes every call that matters.

  1. Bring in the brand

    Enter your website (and any sister sites). Cutfish reads the pages and drafts your brand profile and audience: what you offer, who it's for, what you can claim and what you never say. You approve every line.

  2. Plan the month

    Set a theme and posting days. Cutfish proposes a month of ideas from your brand, audience and current trends, each with a first-frame hook and one thing it tests, and fills the open days on your calendar.

  3. Script every post

    One click drafts the voiceover, on-screen text, caption and hashtags. The hook gets checked against what stops the scroll, and hashtags come as a broad, niche and trending mix.

  4. Make the video

    Cutfish writes a ready-to-paste prompt for each line, naming the avatar to use in Google Vids. Finished clips arrive from a shared Drive folder, find their lines, and render as a 9:16 video with captions, an end card and an AI label.

  5. Review before it ships

    A reviewer checks every claim against what the brand can back, flags health topics for an expert, and confirms the AI label. Nothing moves on without that sign-off.

  6. Post and learn

    Approved videos go to your TikTok account as drafts for the owner to post. Reels get the same finished video and caption. Views, retention and sign-ups come back into the next month's plan.

Safety and control

AI drafts. People decide.

Short-form content for health, wellness and consumer brands has rules. Cutfish is built so that nothing reaches your audience without a person signing off.

Nothing posts by itself

Videos go to your TikTok account as drafts. The account owner opens them in TikTok and posts. Cutfish never publishes without a person.

Claims you can back

Every script is checked against the claims your brand can make and the words it never uses, along with platform health-claim rules. Issues come with a safer rewrite.

Labeled as AI

Avatar videos carry a visible AI-generated label, and the reviewer confirms TikTok's AI-content setting before posting. Avatars are synthetic, never a real person's likeness.

Your accounts stay yours

You connect TikTok and Google on their own sign-in pages, so we never see your passwords. Access tokens are encrypted, Drive access is read-only, and you can disconnect at any time.
